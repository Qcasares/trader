# Roadmap: trader

## Overview
An automated crypto trading bot that combines technical indicators with social sentiment analysis to generate and execute trade signals via the bankr.bot API and CLI. Three data-collection agents (Research, Sentiment, Technical) are complete. The roadmap covers the remaining decision-making agents, trade execution layer, orchestration, and testing needed to reach a working dry-run system.
